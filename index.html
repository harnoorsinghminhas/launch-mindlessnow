This is a Mindless Now pause. One minute, between one prompt and the next.

Stop typing. Let your hands rest on the desk. Feel the surface under your palms.

Take a slow breath in. And a slower breath out.

You have been inside a screen, talking to a machine that answers instantly. Your own mind does not need to be that fast.

Breathe in again. Let the out breath carry your shoulders down.

Notice the room around you. A sound. The light. The air on your skin.

You are here, and nothing is waiting for you in this moment.

One more breath.

When you are ready, go back to your work, a little slower, and a little clearer.
